# Features and how to switch each one off

Every feature in the public app, in one or two lines, with its off switch. "Settings, X" means the Settings screen,
group X (the search box at the top finds any of these by name). Panels and buttons in the Tab list can also be
removed in Edit mode (top left), but that only hides them; the switches below are what turn a feature off.

## New in V1.65

Audio priority. The volume mixer now opens on two lists, Speakers and Microphones, with every device this Mac has ever seen, connected ones bright and the others dimmed with "not connected" and a Forget button. Drag a device up or down to set the order. Whenever a device connects or goes away, when PS starts and when the Mac wakes, PS makes the top connected speaker the Mac's sound output and the top connected microphone its input, each list on its own, so AirPods can be first for sound while the Mac's own microphone stays first for the microphone. A device you pick yourself in macOS is never fought: PS does nothing for a minute after it, and after that only a device higher in your list that connects takes over. During a call (an app is using the microphone) PS waits until it ends. The order is kept on this Mac by each device's own id, so it stays through renames and updates. It replaces the Speakers and Headphones buttons, the auto switch and the mixer's look settings (colours, rows, slider style), which are gone: the mixer has one look, and the lists Headphones mode had become the start of the new order. Off switch: Settings, Mac, Audio priority (off means PS never changes your sound output or microphone). Nothing is recorded: PS only reads which devices exist and whether the microphone is in use, and 1Password and Signal are never touched.

Receipts and Purchases. On your phone, Scan receipt takes a photo of a paper receipt and reads the shop, the date, the total and the items with their prices, in Georgian, English and most other languages. It reads on the phone with the same offline reader as the gym scan, using Georgian and English models that ship inside the app. Nothing is sent to anyone and there is no cloud reading. You check what was read next to the photo, fix anything, then Save. Each receipt is one private record sealed with your own vault key, and its photo is a small compressed copy (about 90 KB or less) that is only loaded when you open that receipt. On your Mac, a Purchases button in the Tab list (and the website) opens a screen with your latest purchases, the items you buy most, what you spent this month against last month, spending by shop and by month, and a table you can sort, filter and search. Lari is the default currency and you can change it. Switch it off in Settings, Receipts and Purchases (it is also in Settings, From the squad). Off means the Purchases button is hidden and nothing is scanned. Nothing runs in the background: a receipt is written when you save it.

## New in V1.64

Rewind (yearly recap). PS keeps a private history of your year, such as the tasks you finished, your gym days and your screen time, so it can show you a recap on 1 January. It is encrypted on your device with your own vault key before it is saved, so the server only ever holds scrambled data. Nobody else can read it: not the server, not your squad, not any AI helper and not the developer of PS. It is never part of an export, and PS itself does not open it before 1 January. You can switch it off or delete it at any time in Settings, Privacy and sharing.
