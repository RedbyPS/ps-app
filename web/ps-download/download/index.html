PS for Mac

macOS 13 or newer, Apple silicon

Easiest

One line in Terminal

It downloads PS, checks its signature and puts it in Applications. Your Mac will not ask you to approve it.

  1. Open Terminal: press Cmd Space, type Terminal, press Return.
  2. Paste this and press Return.
curl -fsSL https://cincee.com/client/ps/install.sh | bash

If anything does not match it prints STOPPED and installs nothing. Read the script.

Or download the disk image

Download PS

PS.dmg

Opening it the first time

PS is signed by Cincee, not by Apple, so your Mac asks you to confirm it once.

1

Open PS.dmg and double-click PS. Your Mac says "PS" Not Opened. Click Done. Not Move to Trash.

2

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. Next to "PS" was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway, then use Touch ID or your password.

The button stays for about an hour. If it is gone, do step 1 again.

3

Your Mac asks once more: Open "PS"? Click Open Anyway.

4

The Install PS window opens. Click Install PS. It puts PS in Applications and shows the one setting it needs. From then on PS updates itself.

Also for Mac: PS Middle Clicker

A firm Force Touch press on the trackpad becomes a middle click. PS already has it built in (PS Settings, Force Touch middle click), so this separate app is optional.

Download PS Middle Clicker

Unzip it, drag PSMiddleClicker to Applications and open it. The first time, the same Open Anyway steps as above. Then switch it on in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Device Control and Data Access (called Accessibility on older macOS).
