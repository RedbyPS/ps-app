    Two switches in System Settings

    Turn PS app (macOS may list it as PS app) on in each, then come back here. The ticks appear by themselves.

    Where should your list live?

    You can change this later, and nothing is lost when you do.

    Keep everything on this Mac

    No account and no Supabase. Your list is encrypted and stays on this Mac. Everything that does not need a server works.

    Sync with your own Supabase

    Your list on your phone and your other Macs too. PS sets up a free Supabase project of your own in about two minutes. In a project that already holds your own app, PS stays in its own separate part and never touches your tables, functions or users.
